## 1. Revenue growth

- Insight: Q2 FY27 INR growth of 11.2% YoY was far above 0.5% QoQ constant-currency growth, and the findings contain no constant-currency YoY figure for Q2 FY27 [S1, 2026-10-08; S4, 2026-10-08].
- Q2 FY27 (quarter ended Sep 2026): consolidated revenue Rs 73,188 crore, up 11.2% YoY in INR and 1.3% QoQ [S1, 2026-10-08; corroborated by S4, 2026-10-08].
- Q2 FY27 constant-currency growth: 0.5% QoQ [S1, 2026-10-08].
- Q1 FY27 (Apr to Jun 2026): revenue about Rs 72,275 crore, up 13.9% YoY and 2.2% QoQ in INR. Search snippet, page not fetched [S2, approx. 2026-07].
- Q1 FY27 constant-currency growth: about 3% YoY and 0.4% QoQ. Search snippet [S2, approx. 2026-07].
- Q1 FY27 USD revenue: about USD 7,624M, flat QoQ and up 2.7% YoY. Not verified against the primary release [S2, approx. 2026-07].
- FY2026 (year ended 31 Mar 2026), company-reported: revenue growth 4.6% YoY on a reported basis. Constant-currency revenue declined 2.4%, which the report attributes mainly to one large Indian transformation programme ending [L1, p.24, FY2026].
- FY2026 five-year revenue CAGR: 10.2% [L1, p.24, FY2026].
- FY2026 currency basis of the 4.6% figure is not stated explicitly in the report [L1, p.24, FY2026; report-analyst flag].
- FY2025-26 constant-currency revenue growth, broker estimate: 9.5% [L2, p.1 line 6, 2026-09-15]. This conflicts with the company figure; see Conflicts.

## 2. AI services

- Insight: On the press-reported basis, annualised AI revenue moved from USD 2.6B in Q1 FY27 to USD 3.1B in Q2 FY27, above 10% of revenue. The FY2026 baseline of USD 2.3B has no stated measurement date, so the series cannot be placed on one timeline [S3, 2026-07-10; S4, 2026-10-08; L1, p.14, FY2026].
- FY2026, company-reported: annualised AI revenue US$ 2.3B, presented as an acceleration metric [L1, p.14 and p.23, FY2026].
- FY2026 measurement date for the USD 2.3B figure: not stated [L1, p.14 and p.23, FY2026].
- FY2026 AI revenue as a share of total revenue: not given. "Annualised" is a run rate, not recognised revenue for the period [L1, p.14 and p.23, FY2026; report-analyst flag].
- FY2026 strategy: "Full Stack AI Services", built across five layers [L1, p.11, FY2026].
- Q1 FY27: annualised AI revenue run rate crossed USD 2.6B. TCV for the quarter was USD 9.5B [S3, 2026-07-10].
- Q1 FY27 annualised AI revenue growth of 13.6% QoQ. Search snippet, not verified [S3 basis, 2026-07-10; S2 snippet, approx. 2026-07].
- Q2 FY27: annualised AI revenue surpassed USD 3.1B, over 10% of total revenue [S4, 2026-10-08; corroborated by S1, 2026-10-08].
- Q2 FY27 TCV: USD 9.6B, slightly above Q1's USD 9.5B [S4, 2026-10-08].
- Broker view: qualitative AI comment only, no figure [L2, 2026-09-15; report-analyst review].

## 3. Headcount and attrition

- Insight: Reported net headcount grew for a third consecutive quarter to 598,056 at Q2 FY27, with trailing 12-month attrition at 13.3%. The company FY2026 total of 617,437 and the broker estimate of about 450,000 are on different bases and must not be compared directly [S5, 2026-10-08; L1, p.108, FY2026; L2, p.1 line 5, 2026-09-15].
- Q2 FY27 headcount: 598,056, net addition 4,258, third consecutive quarter of net growth. Attributed to TCS's quarterly fact sheet of 8 Oct 2026 [S5, 2026-10-08]. Headcount corroborated by S1, 2026-10-08.
- Q2 FY27 trailing 12-month attrition: 13.3%, versus 13.6% in the prior quarter [S5, 2026-10-08]. The same article also says attrition "remained stable at 13.3%"; see Conflicts.
- Q1 FY27 net addition: 9,279. Search snippet only, not verified [S5 confidence note, 2026-10-08].
- Q3 FY26 (Oct to Dec 2025): headcount 582,163, down from 593,314. Trailing 12-month attrition 13.5%. Search snippet, page not fetched, figures not corroborated [S6, approx. 2026-01].
- Q2 FY26 (Jul to Sep 2025, before the window): headcount fell by 19,755. The union NITES disputes this as underreporting. This is a union allegation, not a company finding [S6 context, approx. 2026-01].
- FY2026 total employees at 31 Mar 2026: 617,437, of which 584,519 permanent. The total includes "Other than Permanent" staff [L1, p.108, FY2026].
- FY2026 voluntary attrition, IT services only: 13.7%. Total-company attrition is not stated in the extracted text [L1, p.55, FY2026].
- A second total of 636,833 appears in a comparison column on p.129. It is probably the FY2025 figure, but the table headings are unclear and this is unconfirmed [L1, p.129, FY2026].
- Broker estimate: total headcount about 450,000 at end of FY2025-26, after higher attrition in the second half. No attrition rate given [L2, p.1 line 5, 2026-09-15].

## Conflicts

- FY2026 constant-currency revenue growth:
  - Company-reported: minus 2.4% [L1, p.24, FY2026].
  - Broker estimate: plus 9.5% [L2, p.1 line 6, 2026-09-15].
  - Possible explanation: broker estimate versus company figure, and different definitions or base periods. The broker's basis is not stated. Neither figure is preferred.
- FY2026 growth, reported versus constant currency:
  - Company: plus 4.6% reported [L1, p.24, FY2026].
  - Company: minus 2.4% constant currency, same page [L1, p.24, FY2026].
  - Possible explanation: currency effect. The report does not state the currency basis of the 4.6% figure explicitly.
- Headcount at end of FY2026:
  - Company total: 617,437 [L1, p.108, FY2026].
  - Company permanent: 584,519 [L1, p.108, FY2026].
  - Broker estimate: about 450,000 [L2, p.1 line 5, 2026-09-15].
  - Derived gaps (arithmetic on the figures above): about 167,000 against the total, about 134,500 against permanent staff.
  - Possible explanation: broker estimate versus company disclosure, and different definitions (inclusion of non-permanent staff, measurement date). Not resolved.
- Two company totals on p.129:
  - 617,437 [L1, p.108, FY2026].
  - 636,833 comparison column [L1, p.129, FY2026].
  - Possible explanation: a prior-year comparative. Unconfirmed because table headings are unclear.
- Q2 FY27 attrition wording within one source:
  - "Fell to 13.3% from 13.6%" [S5, 2026-10-08].
  - "Remained stable at 13.3%" [S5, 2026-10-08].
  - Possible explanation: internal inconsistency in the article. Not resolved.
- Attrition basis:
  - Q2 FY27 trailing 12-month attrition: 13.3% [S5, 2026-10-08].
  - FY2026 IT services voluntary attrition: 13.7% [L1, p.55, FY2026].
  - Possible explanation: different periods, total versus IT services only, and trailing 12-month versus fiscal-year basis.
  - Broker says attrition was higher in the second half of FY2026 [L2, 2026-09-15]. No Q4 FY26 trailing figure is in the findings, so the direction cannot be checked.
- Q1 FY27 revenue:
  - Rs 72,275 crore [S2, approx. 2026-07].
  - Rs 59,553 crore [Sahi.com, date unknown]. Excluded as inconsistent with the other sources.
  - Possible explanation: unknown. The findings do not identify the basis of the Sahi.com figure.
- Q1 FY27 net profit:
  - Reported as Rs 13,349 crore in some outlets and Rs 13,849 crore in others [Angel One and Bajaj Broking snippets, approx. 2026-07].
  - Possible explanation: different reporting definitions or outlet errors. Profit excluded from this report.
- Q2 FY27 USD revenue:
  - One search summary gave a figure of about USD 7,642M. A second search summary gave a figure with a typo. Neither is used [S1 basis, 2026-10-08].
  - Possible explanation: search-summary error. Not verified against the primary release.
- Q3 FY26 window status:
  - The brief describes Q3 FY26 as outside the strict window [S6 basis, approx. 2026-01].
  - By its quarter dates (Oct to Dec 2025), Q3 FY26 falls inside the window [S6, approx. 2026-01].
  - Possible explanation: a labelling inconsistency in the brief. Treated here as in-window but unverified.
- AI run rate across periods:
  - FY2026 report: USD 2.3B, no measurement date [L1, p.14 and p.23, FY2026].
  - Q1 FY27: USD 2.6B [S3, 2026-07-10].
  - Q2 FY27: USD 3.1B [S4, 2026-10-08].
  - Possible explanation: not a contradiction, but the different measurement dates and undated baseline prevent a clean series. Any step between the figures cannot be checked against the 13.6% QoQ snippet.
