**TCS: research summary (window: last 12 months, as of 26 Oct 2026)**

Q2 FY27 shows sequential revenue recovery with flat margins. Constant-currency revenue declined in FY26, AI remains a stated aspiration without disclosed revenue, and attrition is 13.7%.

**Revenue growth**
- Q2 FY27 revenue from operations was ₹73,188 crore, up 1.3% QoQ and 11.2% YoY. Source: IndiaInfoline, 9 Oct 2026. Search summaries of other outlets report the same figure; Yourstory returned HTTP 403 and was not read.
- Q2 FY27 consolidated net profit was ₹13,884 crore, up 4% QoQ. Reported YoY growth is about 15%, but the base is distorted by a Q1 exceptional legal-settlement loss. Excluding it, profit rose only marginally. Source: IndiaInfoline, 9 Oct 2026; the 15% figure is from search summaries.
- Operating margin was 24%, flat QoQ and about 120 bps lower YoY. Source: search summary only, not verified on the fetched page.
- Dividend of ₹12 per share, record date 14 Oct 2026, payment 30 Oct 2026. Source: IndiaInfoline, 9 Oct 2026. The article does not label it interim.
- FY26 revenue grew 4.6% in INR but declined 2.4% in constant currency, mainly because one large India transformation programme ended. Five-year revenue CAGR is 10.2% (management's metric). Source: TCS Integrated Annual Report 2025-26, p.24. Publication date not stated in the extract.
- Sector signal: Infosys cut its FY27 revenue growth guidance to 1-2% from 1.5-3%, citing pricing pressure. Source: Latestly, 26 Oct 2026. Single source, not checked against Infosys's filing.

**AI services**
- Management aspires to become the world's largest AI-led technology services company, with a full-stack strategy from infrastructure to intelligence across five pillars. Source: TCS Annual Report 2025-26, p.10. This is a forward-looking ambition, not a measured result.
- No AI revenue, AI bookings, or AI deal-count disclosure was found in either source.

**Headcount and attrition**
- Voluntary IT services attrition was 13.7% in FY26, with management stating it maintained a high talent retention rate. Source: TCS Annual Report 2025-26, p.55. FY25 and FY24 comparisons were not re-verified.
- Principal risk: competition for specialised AI talent may raise attrition. Mitigations are role-based AI reskilling for every employee, retention programmes, and tier-2/tier-3 delivery centres. Source: TCS Annual Report 2025-26, p.63.
- No total headcount or quarterly net headcount change was found.

**Governance context (Tata Sons, not TCS)**
- N Chandrasekaran will not seek reappointment as Tata Sons chairman when his term ends on 20 Feb 2027, and has asked the board to begin succession planning. Source: Moneylife, 12 Aug 2026, search summary. Sources differ on details, and the excerpt was not verified on the fetched page.
