# TCS: Research for Paper-Trading Watchlist

Research only. Paper-trading context. Not a buy or sell recommendation.

## 1. Key insights

- Reported revenue growth accelerated in FY27 quarters, to +13.9% YoY in Q1 and +11.2% YoY in Q2, after reported FY26 growth of only 4.6% [S2, 2026-07-10; S1, 2026-10-08; S5, FY2026, undated].
- The FY26 constant-currency figure (-2.4%) conflicts directly with a broker estimate of +9.5% for the same year [S5, FY2026, undated; S6, 2026-09-15]. Resolve before using any growth narrative.
- Reported AI revenue run rate rose from US$2.3bn (FY26, annual report) to above US$2.6bn (Q1 FY27) and US$3.1bn (Q2 FY27) [S5, FY2026, undated; S2, 2026-07-10; S1, 2026-10-08]. The periods differ, but the direction is consistent across sources.
- Headcount has risen for three consecutive quarters to 598,056, with trailing attrition easing to 13.3% [S1, 2026-10-08].
- The broker headcount estimate of about 450,000 is irreconcilable with the company-reported figures and should be treated as an outlier until explained [S6, 2026-09-15; S5, FY2026, undated; S1, 2026-10-08].
- Sell-side ratings are split: one Buy with a Rs 2,400 target, one Underperform, one Sell [S4, 2026-10].

## 2. Revenue growth

- Q2 FY27 (quarter to 30 Sep 2026): revenue from operations Rs 73,188 crore, +11.22% YoY from Rs 65,799 crore [S1, 2026-10-08].
- Q2 FY27 consolidated net profit Rs 13,934 crore, +14.86% YoY [S1, 2026-10-08].
- Q1 FY27 (quarter to 30 Jun 2026): revenue Rs 72,275 crore, +13.9% YoY [S2, 2026-07-10].
- FY2026 (year to 31 Mar 2026): reported growth 4.6%; constant-currency revenue -2.4%, which the company attributes largely to one large India transformation programme ending [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 24].
- FY2026 consolidated revenue US$30bn [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 5].
- Five-year revenue CAGR to FY2026: 10.2% [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 24].
- Note on periods: the annual report covers FY2026 (to 31 Mar 2026). The news items cover Q1 and Q2 FY27 (Apr to Sep 2026). These are different periods and should not be read as one trend line.
- Note on definitions: the news items do not state whether their growth rates are reported or constant-currency. The annual report distinguishes the two.

## 3. AI services

- Annualised AI revenue, FY2026 (based on latest quarterly revenue): US$2.3bn [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 5; also p. 8].
- Annualised AI revenue run rate, Q1 FY27: above US$2.6bn [S2, 2026-07-10].
- Annualised AI revenue, Q2 FY27: US$3.1bn, above 10% of total revenue, up from the Q1 run rate [S1, 2026-10-08].
- The company states an aspiration to become the world's largest AI-led technology services company [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 5 and p. 8].
- Broker view: AI-related deals are a small share of total bookings so far in FY26; no figure given [S6, 2026-09-15, line 7].
- Comparability: the annual report figure is a revenue measure; the broker comment concerns bookings. They cannot be compared [S6, 2026-09-15, line 7; S5, FY2026, undated, p. 5].
- Implied share (derived, not reported by any source): US$2.3bn against US$30bn full-year revenue is about 7.7%. This uses full-year revenue as the denominator, so it is not directly comparable to the ">10%" share in Q2 FY27 [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 5; S1, 2026-10-08].
- The Q1 FY27 deal-win headline (US$9.5bn) appears in a source title only and is not confirmed in the body text [S2, 2026-07-10, title].

## 4. Headcount and attrition

- Headcount, Sep 2025 quarter: 593,314 [S3, 2026-01-12].
- Headcount, Dec 2025 quarter: 582,163, a net decline of 11,151 [S3, 2026-01-12].
- Headcount, FY2026 (period end not stated): 584,519 [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 23].
- Headcount, Sep 2026 quarter: 598,056, with net addition of 4,258 in Q2 FY27, the third consecutive quarter of net addition [S1, 2026-10-08].
- Derived: implied headcount at the June 2026 quarter is about 593,798 (598,056 less 4,258). This is my arithmetic from S1 and has not been reported by any source.
- Attrition, trailing 12 months, Sep 2026 quarter: 13.3%, down from 13.6% in the prior quarter [S1, 2026-10-08].
- Attrition, LTM, Dec 2025 quarter: 13.5% in IT services [S3, 2026-01-12].
- Voluntary LTM attrition, IT services, FY2026 (end date not stated): 13.7% [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 25].
- Fresher hiring reportedly doubled in Q3 FY26 [S3, 2026-01-12, title only].
- Broker estimate: headcount about 450,000 at end FY26, after higher second-half attrition; no attrition figure given [S6, 2026-09-15, line 5].

## 5. Conflicts

- **FY2026 constant-currency revenue growth**
  - -2.4%: annual report, FY2026 [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 24]
  - +9.5% (estimate): broker note, FY2025-26 [S6, 2026-09-15, line 6]
  - Possible explanations: company actual versus third-party estimate; different base or scope; the broker figure may not be a like-for-like constant-currency measure. The findings do not say which.
- **Q2 FY27 growth**
  - +11.22% YoY reported: news report [S1, 2026-10-08]
  - +2.8% YoY constant currency (unverified): aggregator [S4, 2026-10]
  - Possible explanations: reported versus constant-currency basis; aggregator error. Unverified.
- **Total headcount**
  - 584,519 (FY2026, annual report) [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 23]
  - About 450,000 (estimate, end FY26) [S6, 2026-09-15, line 5]
  - 582,163 (Dec 2025 quarter) [S3, 2026-01-12]
  - Possible explanations: estimate versus disclosed figure; different period ends; the broker figure may be wrong. The gap of roughly 135,000 is too large to be a rounding or timing difference.
- **Headcount disclosure within one source**
  - The S3 article states that TCS did not disclose total headcount for the quarter, yet reports 582,163 for that quarter [S3, 2026-01-12].
  - Possible explanation: the figure may come from a source other than the company's quarterly disclosure. Not resolved in the findings.
- **Attrition definitions**
  - 13.3% trailing 12 months, overall [S1, 2026-10-08]
  - 13.5% LTM, IT services [S3, 2026-01-12]
  - 13.7% voluntary LTM, IT services [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 25]
  - Possible explanations: different periods; voluntary versus total attrition; IT services versus group scope.
- **AI revenue comparability**
  - Annual report: US$2.3bn annualised AI revenue [S5, FY2026, undated, p. 5]
  - Broker: AI deals are a small share of bookings, no figure [S6, 2026-09-15, line 7]
  - Possible explanation: revenue versus bookings measures. Not directly comparable.
- **Source identity**
  - The broker note is attributed to "Example Securities" [S6, 2026-09-15]. Verify the publisher before relying on it.
- **Sell-side targets**
  - Motilal Oswal: Buy, target Rs 2,400 [S4, 2026-10]
  - Jefferies: Underperform, no target given [S4, 2026-10]
  - Ambit Capital: Sell [S4, 2026-10]
  - Aggregator average target: about Rs 2,324 across 25 targets [S4, 2026-10]
  - Possible explanation: differing views and dates. Ratings are third-party opinions, not recommendations.
